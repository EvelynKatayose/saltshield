SaltShield — Wachusett Planning Workspace

Public website: https://evelynkatayose.github.io/saltshield/
Demo entry: https://evelynkatayose.github.io/saltshield/?demo=1
No login is required.
Demo mode starts with $75,000, preserves ordinary saved work, provides two budget presets, and includes Restart demo in both interfaces.

Open results/SaltShield_Wachusett.html in a browser. Everything needed to run the app is embedded in that file; no account or network connection is required.
The default entry is an immersive winter experience: brush the snow with the cursor, follow Snow / Salt / Water, scatter illustrated salt and create ripples. Pause motion is available, and reduced-motion preferences are respected. The landscape and particles are illustrative artwork, not a water-quality simulation.
Scroll to the live budget and coverage comparison. Moving its budget slider updates the planning workspace. Switch Independent / Shared, drag the map divider and hover or focus a town. Existing cost, objective, effect and reach edits still apply. Open planner enters the detailed Road Coverage workspace; the SaltShield brand returns to the experience.

Overview: watershed map, town inventory and the live decision brief.
Investments: edit budget, salt baseline, project costs, coverage and enablement; compare independent and shared funding.
Investments also compares no new investment, independent optimization, benefit/cost ordering and the pooled optimum. Choose Zone A or all A/B/C roads as the objective. Inspect each project's selection reason and its selection across 27 assumed cases; export comparison and sensitivity CSVs.
Road Coverage: link equipment purchases to whole road cells under a simulated service-reach limit. Edit six reach assumptions, compare funding rules, inspect assignments and export linked JSON or cell CSV. The standalone four-method capacity experiment remains below it.
Road Coverage also checks nine CAL/BRINE capacity cases. Changed inputs clear old checks; Run reach check recalculates them. Download decision brief exports the current feasible linked plan with its map, assumptions, funding comparison, valid reach check and source hashes. A saved default brief is available at results/SaltShield_Linked_Brief.html.
Evidence: DCR historical grants, five winters of regional weather and GIS measurement rules.

Download brief creates a standalone English HTML summary of the selected funding rule and current coverage case. Print brief prints only that summary. Valid settings are saved on this browser/device when local storage is available. JSON scenario import/export remains available in Investments.

Measured inventory: 466.91 km of source-record roads in the three-town watershed intersection; 462.64 km in protection zones A/B/C. Lengths are centerline kilometers, not lane-kilometers or contracted service mileage. Source records retain about 78.8 m of coincident linework.

Historical evidence: 13 DCR awards from FY2021–FY2026, totaling $188,251. Grants are not current equipment quotes or proof of installation. Weather: 18,144 ERA5 hourly records across five complete winters, 2021–2022 through 2025–2026. Regional weather is context only, not road-surface observations or salt-use calibration.

Default investment assumptions: CAL $15,000 / 10% central reduction; BRINE $30,000 / 25%; 5 t of applied salt per centerline km per winter. Prices, effects and equipment coverage are illustrative.

At a $75,000 aggregate ceiling, independent funding selects $45,000 of projects and models 41.54 t of avoided Zone A application. Pooling selects $75,000 and models 84.84 t. At the same $45,000 actual spending, unrestricted pooling models 52.56 t while funding only two towns; requiring every town returns 41.54 t.

Spatial planning uses 173 occupied 1 km cells. At 25% capacity per town, the whole-cell optimum covers 39.56 km of Zone A roads versus 20.77 km under uniform coverage. Cells are not validated maintenance routes. Capacity is illustrative, and spatial coverage is computed independently of the investment budget.

Editable saved inputs: data/parameters/operations_baseline.csv, project_options.csv, scenario_config.json, and project_capacity.csv. The initializers retain existing edits. After changing the first three tables, rerun planning_v1.py, joint_planning.py and build_report.py. For capacity-only edits, rerun joint_planning.py and build_report.py. Per-lane rates, application counts and operating-cost changes remain blank because they are not verified or modeled. Parameter fingerprints prevent stale report builds and mismatched scenario imports.

Linked planning: CAL defaults to 25 km and BRINE to 50 km of unique eligible road centerline per winter; neither is a measured service capacity. Coverage percentage is an additional reach ceiling. Whole-cell demand rounds up to 10 m and capacity rounds down. Reductions use selected measured lengths, town baseline and project effect once. Every-town participation requires nonempty coverage. Reach settings save separately on this device. SciPy benchmarks apply only while all live inputs match the saved inputs. The overview decision brief continues to describe the uniform investment model and standalone coverage case; use the linked exports for equipment/cell decisions.

Source refresh (requires internet): download_data.py, download_evidence.py, download_weather.py. Rebuild all affected outputs afterward. See README.md for exact definitions and limitations.

Before procurement, verify actual municipal salt logs, equipment inventories and available capacity, service requirements, operating costs and vendor quotes. This is a planning demonstrator, not a deployed municipal system, concentration forecast or verified safety outcome.
