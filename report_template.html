MWRA Wachusett Reservoir, SOURCE_ID 6000000-01S. The study includes only its intersections with the three towns, not the entire watershed.

Measurement rules

Lengths are measured in EPSG:26986. Zone A takes priority, followed by B excluding A, then C excluding A/B. Unmatched roads remain separate. Exact town-border linework is assigned once.

Investment eligibility: jurisdiction code 2, paved surface codes 3–9, excluding bicycle/pedestrian facilities. Unknown, unaccepted and state roads remain in the inventory.

Investment results estimate avoided application under assumptions, not delivered chloride loads, water concentrations or verified road-safety outcomes.
