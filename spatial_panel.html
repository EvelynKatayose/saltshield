How coverage is compared

Whole cells and partial cells

The optimum uses binary integer planning; the heuristic sorts cells by their Zone A share. Both select whole cells. Uniform coverage assumes the same fractional share on every road, while the fractional upper bound permits partial cells. These two cases are benchmarks, not executable whole-cell plans. Unused capacity is retained.

Planning limits

Capacity percentages are examples, not measured fleet capacity. Cells are not validated maintenance routes. Travel links, weather concurrency, labor, storage and safety constraints are not modeled. Coverage is independent of the equipment-budget comparison and is not a measured salt saving.

The grid uses EPSG:26986 whole-kilometer coordinates and is clipped to the town/watershed intersections. Splitting conserves road length. Approximately 78.8 m of source-record overlap remains, as in the base inventory.
