November 1–March 31 in America/New_York time. ERA5's approximately 0.25° grid describes regional conditions; it does not measure road-surface temperature. Weather is context only and does not calibrate salt use or equipment benefits.

Definitions and attribution

Cold-wet hours have 2 m air temperature ≤ 2°C and preceding-hour precipitation ≥ 0.1 mm. This descriptive threshold does not diagnose road icing. Precipitation includes rain and snow water equivalent; snowfall depth is a separate variable and cannot be added to it.
